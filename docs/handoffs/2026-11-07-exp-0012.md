## Actual Results
See `docs/experiments/EXP-0012-predictive-state.md` R1-R4 and `results/predictive_state/report.md`. Headline: reactive and recurrent policies are within seed noise of each other at ~0.45-0.65 macro success; the JEPA-trained GRU is the weakest FP arm; among JEPA-trained arms FP16/INT8/INT4/ternary are within seed noise (no precision effect detected; not measured without JEPA); encoder runs can be cut ~60% by even a fixed-rate schedule; ternary vs INT4 is ~2x bytes and 1x MACs analytically.

## Remaining Problems
- Seed variance dominates small gaps (KI-022). Only 3 seeds, fewer for G-I4u (2), G-I8a and G-Ta-s8 (1).
- The INT4/INT8 QAT recipes and the JEPA weight were not tuned; "ternary >= INT4" and "JEPA hurts" are conditional on those recipes.
- The benchmark's blackouts are brief, so persistent state matters only in the exploratory long-blackout conditions. S4's early under-performance disappeared with the larger training budget; no cause was found for the earlier gap.
- Measured ternary latency does not exist (no packed kernel), so no MEASURED claim about ternary speed or energy.

## Important Context
