## Actual Results

See `docs/experiments/EXP-0010-edge-inference-baseline.md` (and the generated `results/edge_inference/summary.md`). In one paragraph: ORT and PyTorch agree exactly; ORT CPU sustains 30 fps at p50 20–24 ms / p99 37–61 ms with 0–28 drops per ~9000 frames; PyTorch eager drops 38–39%; the network is under half of per-frame compute; the detector scores AP50 0.13 (holdout) / 0.20 (development) at IoU 0.5 and 0.34 / 0.32 at IoU 0.1; no energy was measured.

## Remaining Problems

- The control is not validated: the score is entangled with box semantics (visible-extent labels vs whole-person predictions) and, on some scenes, with forklift confusion (KI-013, Q-025).
- Timing is confounded by laptop power state and has rare unexplained stalls (KI-012). Nearly all recorded runs started on battery; an AC set exists, and the machine drained to ~15% charge.
- B1 is an optimized *network* runtime, not an optimized pipeline: decode/pre/post cost more than the network.
- One camera per layout, 5-minute clips, synthetic only; no events, no energy, no other hardware.
