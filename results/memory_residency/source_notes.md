# EXP-0006 source notes

Source quality: six rows are primary documents (S03 AMD ISSCC 2022 slides; S09 d-Matrix Hot Chips 2025 PDF; S12 Micron HBM3E product brief and Samsung newsroom; S13 JEDEC release and Micron white paper; S14 the MICRO-50 paper; S15 the ISSCC 2014 paper). The remaining rows are technical-press or WikiChip/SemiWiki summaries of primary disclosures (S01, S02, S04-S08, S10, S11, S16-S22) and are adequate for order-of-magnitude use only.

Every physical constant in `analysis/memory_residency/` traces to a row of `sources.csv` (external) or `assumptions.csv` (explicit). Tiers are kept apart: raw bit-cell density (S01/S02) is never used as a chip density; macro density (S02) excludes TSVs, routing and compute; cache-only die density (S03/S04) includes TSVs and periphery but no compute; whole SRAM-heavy die density (S05/S06/S07) is the only tier that includes compute and is the tier a FlexTern die is assumed to land in (A1). Vendor bandwidths are peak; utilization factors (A4) are applied. Energy figures are ranges (A3), the SRAM side rests on a 45 nm tutorial (S15) and is labeled accordingly; nothing is summed into a chip J/token.

- **S01** SRAM high-density bit cell (6T), TSMC N5 — TSMC N5 technology (IEDM 2019 / ISSCC 2020 disclosures, summarized by WikiChip) (2020, N5; commercial process; published cell size). reported_area: 0.021 um2 per bit (cell); reported_density: 47.6 Mb/mm2 raw bit-cell (INFERRED = 1/0.021 um2).
